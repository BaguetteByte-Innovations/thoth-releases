## Preamble

The Publisher develops Thoth, an identity management and synchronisation software suite for the education sector, made up of Thoth Engine, Thoth StorageSteward and Thoth Campus (hereinafter "the Software").

The Software is under development and has not yet been placed on the market. The Publisher wishes to have it evaluated under real conditions, as part of a beta programme. The Client wishes to use it on an experimental basis and free of charge, in return for its feedback.

The Parties have agreed as follows.

## Article 1 — Purpose

The Publisher makes the Software available to the Client free of charge, as part of its beta programme, for the sole purposes of testing it and assessing its suitability for the Client's needs.

This Agreement constitutes neither a sale, nor a promise of sale, nor a commitment to market the Software. It grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sublicensable licence to use the Software, limited to the Client's own organisation and to the term defined in Article 4.

The person submitting the application declares that they are authorised to bind the Client, or that they act with the consent of its representative.

The Publisher reviews each application and remains free to accept or refuse it, without having to give reasons. This Agreement enters into force on the date on which the Publisher accepts the application and makes the licence certificate available to the Client.

The text of this Agreement remains available for consultation and printing on the thoth-iam.com website throughout the beta programme.

The Parties may also conclude this Agreement on paper, signed by each of them. It then enters into force when the licence certificate is made available.

## Article 4 — Term and licence certificate

The term of the right of use is set by an electronic licence certificate supplied by the Publisher, which alone determines its expiry date.

The certificate is valid until the expiry date it bears, which marks the end of the beta programme. This date is the same for all participants, regardless of the date on which this Agreement entered into force. Any extension, like the issue of a new certificate, is at the Publisher's sole discretion and places no obligation on the Publisher.

When the certificate expires, and after a grace period of thirty (30) days, the Software stops allowing creation and modification operations; viewing and deletion remain possible. The Client alone determines the environment in which it deploys the Software, including the choice between a production and a test environment. This choice is the Client's sole responsibility.

Before any installation, the Client undertakes to:

- a) make a full backup of its directory and of the systems concerned, and make sure that this backup can actually be restored;
- b) check that the Software is compatible with its technical environment;
- c) appoint a technical contact person to liaise with the Publisher.

The Client ensures the security, operation and administration of its infrastructure. It is the Client's responsibility to check the effects of the operations performed by the Software before and after they are carried out.

## Article 6 — Personal data

The Software runs entirely on the Client's infrastructure. The Publisher has no access to any data processed by the Software, collects no usage data and operates no telemetry. The licence certificate is verified locally, without connecting to any remote service.

Consequently, the Publisher acts neither as controller nor as processor within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. The Client remains solely responsible for the processing carried out by means of the Software and for the resulting obligations, in particular regarding information of data subjects and security.

Defects are reported exclusively by means of reconstructed, non-identifying data sets, to the exclusion of any extraction of production data. No personal data may be sent to the Publisher. Should any be sent, the Publisher deletes it immediately.
